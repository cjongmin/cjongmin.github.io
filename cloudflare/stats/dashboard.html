Where visitors are

Cloudflare's estimate from the IP address, not GPS: often the centre of the city or the provider's hub, so people in one city can share a spot. Zoomed in, each visitor gets a dot, and dots sharing a spot fan out around it. Someone back on another day gets a new dot. Rings mark the last 5 minutes.

Live

Every visit and click as it arrives, Korea time.

    Institutions

    The organization that owns the visitor's network, not the person's affiliation. Campus networks show the school; the same person at home shows their provider.

    Visit paths

    What one visitor did, in order. Steps are joined by a code from their network and browser that changes daily, and 30 minutes of silence ends a visit. Changing network or passing midnight in Korea starts a new path, and people who share a network and browser can appear as one.

광고 (무료 이용 시) — Google AdMob

무료 기능의 광고는 Google AdMob(Google LLC, 미국)이 게재합니다. 광고 게재·성과 측정· 부정 방지를 위해 Google이 다음 정보를 처리할 수 있습니다: 기기 광고 식별자(IDFA — 추적 허용 시에만), IP 주소로 추정한 대략적 위치, 광고 노출·클릭 등 상호작용, 기기·앱 진단 정보. 이 정보는 Google 서버(미국 등 국외)로 전송되어 Google의 정책에 따라 보관됩니다.

  • 추적(IDFA)은 iOS 앱 추적 투명성(ATT)에서 허용한 경우에만 사용되며, iPhone 설정 → 개인정보 보호 및 보안 → 추적에서 언제든 바꿀 수 있습니다.
  • 유럽경제지역·영국·스위스 사용자는 처음 표시되는 동의 양식에서 선택하며, 앱의 설정 → "광고 개인정보 설정"에서 언제든 변경·철회할 수 있습니다.
  • 동의하지 않아도 비맞춤 광고로 모든 기능을 이용할 수 있으며, Pro 구독 중에는 광고가 표시되지 않습니다.

Privacy Policy

Data the developer collects: none

All video analysis, editing, and exporting in PickCam happens entirely on your device. The developer does not collect, transmit, or store your videos, photos, facial data, or any personal information. There are no accounts and no sign-in. Projects you are editing (including a copy of the video) are kept only in the app's storage on this device, are excluded from iCloud backup, and are removed when you delete the project or the app.

About face recognition

To track a person, PickCam temporarily computes visual facial features in device memory during analysis. This does not identify who you are, never leaves your device, and is discarded when editing ends.

Advertising (free tier) — Google AdMob

Ads in the free tier are served by Google AdMob (Google LLC, USA). To serve and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google may process: your device's advertising identifier (IDFA — only if you allow tracking), approximate location inferred from your IP address, ad interactions such as impressions and clicks, and device and app diagnostics. This data is transferred to Google's servers (including outside your country) and handled under Google's policies.

  • Tracking (IDFA) is used only if you allow it under iOS App Tracking Transparency; you can change this anytime in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Users in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland choose in the consent form shown at first launch and can change or withdraw consent anytime in the app's Settings → "Ad Privacy Settings".
  • If you decline, you still get full functionality with non-personalized ads. No ads are shown while you are subscribed to Pro.

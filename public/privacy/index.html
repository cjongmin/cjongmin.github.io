Privacy

This covers cjmin.me and world.cjmin.me. The iOS apps have their own pages under /app/.

What is counted

To see which work people read and roughly where they read it from, this site records each page view and some clicks:

  • The page's address and title, the page you came from (without its query string), any source or campaign named in the link, and which paper, code, app or file link you clicked.
  • How long the page was on screen and how far it was scrolled.
  • Your device type, screen width, operating system and browser with their versions, and language setting.
  • An estimated place and the name of the network you are on, which Cloudflare derives from your IP address: country, region, city, coordinates rounded to about 10 km, and the organization that owns the network, such as an internet provider, a company or a university.

What is not kept

  • Your IP address is not stored. It is combined with your browser's identification and a random value that changes every day, and only a shortened scrambled result is kept. Within one day this lets the owner see the order of the pages in a visit, such as the home page and then a talk. The random value is then deleted, so visits on different days cannot be linked.
  • No cookies or similar identifiers are set for visitors, and there are no advertising trackers. The one exception is if you opt out below: one cookie then records only that choice.
  • The site does not ask for your device's location. The place is only an estimate from your network, and it is often just where your provider is.

Who sees it, and for how long

Only the site owner can open these statistics. They are not sold or shared, and they are deleted after 400 days. Cloudflare hosts the site and stores the data. GoatCounter, a cookieless counter, also counts page views.
